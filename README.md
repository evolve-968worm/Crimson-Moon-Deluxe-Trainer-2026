## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Crimson Moon: Deluxe Edition Upgrade**. This trainer enhances Crimson Moon's brutal melee missions by protecting the Nephilim from damage, increasing offensive power, and extending access to supernatural abilities during solo play.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more accessible solo experience, faster combat progression, easier boss encounters, or additional freedom while exploring Crimson Moon's mission-based campaign.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Enter missions from the Church sanctuary and move through the fallen city of Gildenarch while managing enemy encounters and mission objectives. Keep the trainer active when crossing dangerous areas or practicing movement around large groups of enemies.

### Tools & Inventory

Manage equipped weapons, armor, and Nephilim abilities between missions at the Church. The Deluxe Upgrade adds the Ceremonial Armor Set and Axe of the Gods, while the trainer helps you use your chosen equipment more effectively during combat.

### World & Environment

The Church serves as the main sanctuary for upgrades, NPC interactions, and War Table planning. Select missions, review available modifiers, and prepare for higher-risk objectives before beginning a run through Gildenarch.

### Combat & Weapons

Crimson Moon focuses on relentless melee combat against demons, undead, vampires, and Hellgrowth creatures. Combine weapons with supernatural Nephilim powers and angelic transformations, then use the trainer's defensive and offensive options to survive difficult encounters and boss battles.
